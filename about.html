About Cartify

Cartify is a sample online shopping website made for an Information Technology Concepts university assignment. The website presents a small collection of products through separate, connected web pages.

Our aim

Our aim is to make product information easy to find. Visitors can explore the collection, view pictures and use the contact page to share a question.

What this website offers

  • A clear home page
  • A small product collection with prices
  • A gallery of product pictures
  • A contact form layout
